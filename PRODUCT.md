# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Anyone who wants to edit video without paying or downloading an app, ranked roughly in this order:

1. **Short-form creators** making TikTok, Reels and Shorts, often on a phone, who want fast 9:16 edits that still look polished.
2. **First-time, everyday editors** who have never edited video and need something free that does not intimidate them.
3. **Freelancers and semi-pros** who care about 4K60 export, ProRes and owning a clean, watermark-free file.
4. **People priced out of other editors** who hit a paywall or a watermark and want everything unlocked.

Context on arrival: they land from a link or a search, often on a phone, and decide within seconds whether this free editor is legit or another bait-and-switch.

## Product Purpose

Motion (working name) is a full, CapCut-style video editor without AI features that runs entirely in the browser and installs as a PWA. Every feature is free, exports are never watermarked, and rendering happens on the user's own device, so nothing is uploaded.

The landing page exists to make one claim believable: **every pro tool, free, with no watermark and no app store.** Success means a visitor understands that in one screen and clicks through to the editor.

## Brand Personality

**Creative, energized, generous.** (Feeling confirmed by the user: "creative and energized". The three words are an assumption to confirm.)

- Voice: direct, confident and warm. Short sentences. Says exactly what you get and where the limits are, with no hype and no fine print.
- Emotional goal: "I want to start making something right now."
- Visual inspiration: a video-agency landing page with a blush and burgundy palette, a sans with italic serif accents, a curved reel of vertical clips, a giant section word and arch-shaped imagery (`docs/inspo/landing-inspo-1.jpg`). Motion pushes it toward energy and toward the language of an editing timeline.

## Anti-references

(Assumed from our planning conversation. Confirm or correct.)

- **CapCut's own look** or any TikTok-style neon-on-black. Motion should not read as a reskin.
- **Generic SaaS landing pages**: purple gradients, floating blobs, icon-title-text card grids, big-number stat rows.
- **Freemium upsell patterns**: pricing tables, "Pro" badges, locked-feature teasers. None of these exist in the product, so none appear on the page.
- **Copying the inspo's identity**: its name, copy or photos.
- **Over-promising**: claiming 4K works on every phone, or implying ProRes is available before it is built.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work, not the pitch.** The page should look like footage and timelines, the things people will make, rather than describe them in feature bullets.
2. **Generous by default.** Nothing on the page hints at a paid tier. Every tool is shown as included because it is.
3. **Honest limits build trust.** State plainly what depends on the device (4K60) and what is coming (ProRes). Credibility is the product's biggest hurdle.
4. **Energy with craft.** Motion and type should feel alive and kinetic, but precise, like a well-cut edit and never like chaos.
5. **Works for everyone.** A phone on a slow connection gets the same story as a 27-inch monitor.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

(Assumed. Confirm.)

- Target **WCAG 2.2 AA**: text contrast at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large display type.
- Respect **prefers-reduced-motion**: auto-drifting and scroll-scrubbed motion stop, and content is shown in its final state.
- Everything works with keyboard and touch. Visible focus states. Drag interactions are optional, never required.
- Mobile-first performance: compressed images and no autoplaying video on the landing page.
